Runs Kane CLI by TestMu AI (Formerly LambdaTest) tests: one natural language objective, or a suite of committed _test.md files. The step installs Kane CLI (and Chrome, unless the suite runs on HyperExecute), prints a pass/fail verdict, archives the evidence pack and logs under kane-out/, and fails the build when any test fails.

The agent needs Node.js 20 or later with npm.

Pipeline example:

kaneCli credentialsId: 'testmu-ai', objective: 'Go to https://example.com and verify the page title contains Example'
